Clear information about what this website collects, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

01 / Scope

About this policy

This policy explains how Envizon Films (“Envizon”, “we”, “our” or “us”) handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us about a project. It covers this website only. Client, crew, talent and production information may also be governed by separate agreements and production notices.

02 / Information

What we may receive

Information you provide

This website does not currently provide user accounts or an online contact form. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, organisation, brief, budget context and any files or links included in your message.

Basic technical information

Our hosting and delivery providers may process limited technical information needed to serve and protect the website, such as an IP address, browser or device type, requested page, referral information, timestamps and diagnostic logs.

Third-party assets

The website loads fonts from Google and icon files from jsDelivr. Connecting to those services may disclose ordinary request information, including your IP address and browser headers, to those providers under their own privacy terms.

03 / Use

Why we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss potential projects.
  • To prepare proposals, production plans or contracts you request.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website.
  • To maintain appropriate business, financial and legal records.
  • To protect our rights and comply with applicable law.

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies on this website. If that changes, we will update this notice and provide any choices required by applicable law.

04 / Sharing

When information is shared

We may share information with service providers that support hosting, email, storage, professional advice or production delivery; with project collaborators when necessary for an enquiry or agreed production; when required by law; or in connection with a legitimate business reorganisation. We do not sell or rent personal information.

05 / Retention

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence and project records only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which they were collected, ongoing business relationships, record-keeping, dispute resolution, security and applicable legal obligations. Retention periods vary with the nature of the record.

06 / Choices

Your privacy choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may ask for information about our processing, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, withdrawal of consent, or grievance handling. Send a request to hello@envizonfilms.com. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where law permits or requires it.

Applicable Indian data-protection law includes the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and notified Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, subject to their commencement and application.

07 / Security

Security and children

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures appropriate to the information we handle. No internet transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure. This site is intended for a general business audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

08 / Updates

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website, services or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top shows when the current version took effect. Material changes will be presented on this page.

Questions or requests? Email hello@envizonfilms.com.
